Audit Calculation & Data Rules

Understand what is confirmed and what is an estimate.

TRANSPARENCY
  • Purchase Cost: Inventory purchase quantity × currently saved wholesale rate.
  • Incoming MRP Potential: Purchase quantity × saved MRP. This is potential retail value, not confirmed sale.
  • Actual MRP Sales: Inventory sale quantity × saved MRP.
  • Gross Profit Estimate: Sale quantity × (saved MRP − saved wholesale rate).
  • Net Profit Estimate: Gross profit estimate − recorded expenses.
  • Party Statement: Amount-only record. No quantity is assumed. QR / statement auto-fetch will be connected in the next phase.
  • Cash / Online / Rokad comes from Night Cash Received, with Inventory Daily Counter as fallback. Expenses come from the Expense Register.
  • Current saved rates are used; historical wholesale / MRP snapshots are not available in this report.
